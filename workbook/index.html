What’s inside

Our team, then one real project

About 20 minutes. We set up how our team works once, then design one real project with it. We leave with a design we can print, share, and test.

  1. Build our teamWho is on it, person or AI, teammate or tool, and the rules we ask of each other. Set it once, reuse it.
  2. Pick a real projectThe work, the outcome, and how much judgment it needs.
  3. Design the work, phase by phaseFrame, Explore, Refine, Decide, Learn. What people do and don’t do, and what AI does and doesn’t.
  4. Name the handoffsWho makes the call, who may redirect, and who checks AI’s work before it moves on.
  5. Look across the patternAnything that showed up in every phase may belong in how our team works.
  6. Test it, then learnTry it for a week or two, look back together, and bring what we learn into the next project.
