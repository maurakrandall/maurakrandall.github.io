What’s inside

Six steps for one real workflow

About 20 minutes. We bring the work, the workbook asks the questions, and we leave with a one-page design we can print, share, and test.

  1. Pick a workflowWhat we’re trying to do, and what a new teammate would need to know first.
  2. Sort the workWhat AI can automate, what it can augment, and what stays reserved for people.
  3. Choose AI’s roleExecute, explore, or challenge the frame. The role should fit the work.
  4. Design the handoffsWhere a person steps in, what they see, and what they can decide.
  5. Choose our signalsHow we’ll know we’re getting sharper, and how we’ll notice if we’re slipping.
  6. Run a short test, then learnTry it for a week or two, look back together, and carry what we learn into the next cycle.
