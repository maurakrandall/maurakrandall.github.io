Four · The boundary

There isn't a correct amount of AI.

The same content operation needs a different setting for every kind of content it produces.

A specification table
A product description
A promotional claim
A market-specific campaign
An image of the physical object
Human judgmentAI participation

There's an appropriate allocation for this content, this market, this moment and these stakes. The system has to make that boundary visible and adjustable, not fixed in a template.

Five · Two readers

The page now has two readers.

The studio has always produced a page for a person. Increasingly it produces a surface read by both a shopper and an agent shopping on their behalf.

A human shopper
understand · compare · trust

An AI agent, on their behalf

The next authoring problem
How do you create once for an experience that has two readers who need different things from the same page?
The outcome

The goal isn't more content.

A faster studio that produces a worse store is not a win. Measure across the whole loop, not the half of it that got quicker.

Creation

Speed, effort, throughput per launch

Quality

Accuracy, brand fidelity, localization

Experience

Relevance, clarity, usefulness

Commerce

Engagement, conversion, outcome
